# Oasis Infobyte Graphics Design Internship — Task 1: Poster Design

## Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the **Oasis Infobyte Internship Program (OIBSIP)** under the Graphics Design track.

The objective of this task was to design an attractive, visually engaging, and professional poster by applying fundamental graphic design principles. The design focuses on effective visual communication, clear information hierarchy, typography, color harmony, composition, and spacing.

## Tools Used

* **Canva** — Poster creation and layout design
* **Typography** — Font selection and text styling
* **Graphic Design Principles** — Visual hierarchy, composition, color, and spacing

## Design Objectives

* Create an attractive and professional poster.
* Communicate the intended message clearly and effectively.
* Establish a strong visual hierarchy.
* Use readable typography and appropriate font combinations.
* Apply balanced composition and consistent spacing.
* Select colors that complement the design's purpose.
* Maintain a clean and visually appealing layout.

## Key Design Features

* **Clear Visual Hierarchy:** Organizes information according to importance.
* **Readable Typography:** Improves readability and highlights key messages.
* **Balanced Composition:** Arranges visual elements for a cohesive appearance.
* **Appropriate Color Combination:** Creates visual harmony and reinforces the design's message.
* **Professional Layout:** Uses alignment, spacing, and structure effectively.
* **Strong Visual Communication:** Presents information in an engaging and accessible way.

## Design Principles Applied

The poster incorporates the following graphic design principles:

1. **Contrast** — Differentiates important elements and improves visibility.
2. **Alignment** — Creates structure and visual consistency.
3. **Hierarchy** — Guides the viewer's attention through the content.
4. **Proximity** — Groups related information together.
5. **Color Theory** — Supports the visual mood and message.
6. **Typography** — Establishes readability and visual identity.
7. **White Space** — Prevents clutter and improves the overall composition.

The final outcome demonstrates the practical application of graphic design principles to create a visually appealing and professional poster.

# GIR Repair Shop — Logo Design README

## 1. Project Overview

**Brand Name:** GIR
**Industry:** Repair & Maintenance Services
**Project Type:** Logo Design & Brand Identity

GIR is a repair shop focused on providing reliable, professional, and efficient repair services. The logo should communicate **trust, technical expertise, reliability, and quality workmanship** while remaining simple and memorable.

## 2. Logo Objective

The goal is to create a distinctive logo that:

* Establishes GIR as a trustworthy repair business
* Communicates repair, maintenance, and technical expertise
* Looks professional and modern
* Is easy to recognize at small and large sizes
* Works across digital and printed materials
* Can be used on shop signage, uniforms, tools, vehicles, social media, and marketing materials

## 3. Design Direction

The visual identity should be:

**Modern • Strong • Professional • Reliable • Technical • Simple**

The logo should avoid unnecessary complexity and use a clean visual structure that makes the **GIR** name the primary focus.

### Possible Visual Elements

The design may explore repair-related concepts such as:

* Wrench or mechanical tools
* Gear/cog
* Screw or bolt
* Shield representing reliability
* Circuit/technical elements
* Abstract repair or maintenance symbols
* Customized typography using the letters **GIR**

The symbol should complement the name rather than overpower it.

## 4. Color Direction

Suggested color palette:

* **Dark Blue / Navy** — trust, professionalism, reliability
* **Orange** — energy, action, repair, visibility
* **Black / Charcoal** — strength and technical quality
* **White** — simplicity and cleanliness

A primary logo should also be developed in **one-color/black-and-white** so it remains usable in situations where color printing is unavailable.

## 5. Typography

Typography should be:

* Bold
* Clean
* Highly readable
* Modern
* Suitable for signage

A strong sans-serif typeface is recommended.

The word **GIR** should have enough visual weight to remain recognizable from a distance.

## 6. Logo Variations

The final identity should include:

### Primary Logo

Full GIR logo with symbol and wordmark.

### Secondary Logo

Simplified horizontal or stacked version for different layouts.

### Icon / Mark

A standalone symbol that can be used for:

* Social media profile pictures
* App icons
* Favicons
* Uniform embroidery
* Small branding applications

### Monochrome Version

Black and white version for documents, stamps, invoices, and single-color printing.

## 7. Applications

The logo should be designed with practical applications in mind, including:

* Shop signage
* Business cards
* Staff uniforms
* Repair tools/equipment
* Vehicles
* Packaging
* Invoices and receipts
* Social media
* Website
* Promotional materials
* Stickers and labels

## 8. Design Requirements

The final logo should be:

* Original
* Scalable
* Vector-based
* Legible
* Memorable
* Professional
* Easy to reproduce
* Suitable for both digital and physical applications

The design should remain recognizable without relying heavily on gradients, complex effects, or excessive detail.

## 9. Deliverables

Recommended final deliverables:

* AI / SVG — editable vector files
* EPS — print-ready vector
* PDF — high-quality presentation/print version
* PNG — transparent background
* JPG — standard image version

### Color Formats

* RGB for digital use
* CMYK for print use
* Black & white version

## 10. Brand Personality

The GIR brand should feel:

> **Reliable enough to trust. Strong enough to last. Professional enough to recommend.**

The final logo should give customers the immediate impression that **GIR is a skilled, dependable, and professional repair service.**

# Fashionista Magazine — Design Project

## Project Overview

**Fashionista** is a fashion and lifestyle magazine focused on style, beauty, trends, creativity, and modern culture. This project aims to create a visually appealing and professional magazine that captures the latest fashion inspirations through compelling photography, elegant typography, and engaging editorial layouts.

## Project Objectives

* Create a modern and stylish magazine design.
* Establish a recognizable visual identity for Fashionista.
* Showcase fashion trends, beauty tips, and lifestyle inspiration.
* Combine high-quality imagery with clean editorial layouts.
* Deliver an engaging and professional reading experience.

## Target Audience

Fashionista is designed for:

* Fashion enthusiasts
* Young adults and trendsetters
* Beauty and lifestyle lovers
* Fashion designers and creative professionals
* Readers interested in modern style and culture

## Design Direction

* **Style:** Modern, elegant, editorial, and trendy
* **Mood:** Sophisticated, creative, and inspiring
* **Layout:** Clean grids with bold headlines and balanced spacing
* **Typography:** A combination of elegant serif fonts and modern sans-serif fonts
* **Photography:** High-quality fashion, beauty, and lifestyle imagery

## Color Palette

The magazine may explore the following colors:

* **Black:** Elegance and sophistication
* **White:** Simplicity and clarity
* **Soft Pink:** Femininity and modern beauty
* **Beige / Nude:** Luxury and warmth
* **Gold:** Premium editorial appeal

The final palette should remain consistent throughout the magazine while allowing individual articles to have their own visual character.

## Magazine Sections

Fashionista may include the following editorial sections:

1. **Cover Story** — Featured models, designers, or fashion personalities.
2. **Fashion Trends** — Seasonal styles and emerging trends.
3. **Beauty & Skincare** — Beauty routines, makeup, and skincare inspiration.
4. **Style Guide** — Outfit ideas and styling tips.
5. **Designer Spotlight** — Interviews and profiles of fashion designers.
6. **Lifestyle** — Culture, wellness, and modern living.
7. **Accessories** — Jewelry, handbags, shoes, and other fashion essentials.
8. **Editor's Note** — An introduction to each issue.

## Cover Design

The cover should feature:

* The **FASHIONISTA** masthead
* A striking main fashion photograph
* A strong cover headline
* Supporting article headlines
* Issue number, date, and price where applicable
* A clear visual hierarchy that attracts readers

The cover should be bold, recognizable, and visually balanced.

## Typography

Typography should support the magazine's editorial identity.

* **Masthead:** Bold, distinctive, and memorable
* **Main Headlines:** Elegant and attention-grabbing
* **Body Text:** Highly readable and comfortable for long-form content
* **Captions:** Simple and consistent

Font pairings should create contrast without compromising readability.

## Technical Specifications

* **Format:** Print and digital magazine
* **Page Size:** A4 (210 × 297 mm), unless another format is required
* **Print Color Mode:** CMYK
* **Image Resolution:** 300 DPI for print
* **Bleed:** Typically 3 mm, subject to printer requirements
* **Digital Export:** RGB PDF
* **Print Export:** High-quality print-ready PDF

## Deliverables

* Magazine cover design
* Interior page layouts
* Editorial typography system
* Color palette and visual guidelines
* Print-ready PDF
* Digital PDF version
* Editable source files
* Optional magazine mockups for presentation

## Design Principles

* Maintain consistent margins and grid systems.
* Use high-quality, properly licensed images.
* Establish clear headline and body-text hierarchy.
* Balance photography with editorial content.
* Maintain consistency across all pages.
* Use whitespace to create a premium editorial feel.
* Ensure text remains readable in both print and digital formats.

## Project Goal

To develop **Fashionista** into a distinctive fashion magazine that celebrates personal style, beauty, creativity, and contemporary culture through compelling visual storytelling and professional editorial design.
